# Code of conduct

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, project discussions, and interactions undertaken on behalf of this project. It is an original project policy.

## Participation

Treat people with respect. Give specific, evidence-based technical feedback. Welcome questions and corrections, explain decisions, and respect contributors' time and privacy. Disagreement about code or design is acceptable; attacks on people are not.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, impersonation, disclosure of private information, and repeated disruption are unacceptable. Do not use technical discussions to target or exclude people based on identity, background, ability, or experience. Do not retaliate against someone for reporting a concern.

## Confidential reporting and alternate contact

**Pending maintainer verification: public-contributor reporting and an alternate conduct/appeal contact are deferred for this WIP source-only preview. They must be established before public contribution intake or supported library/CLI publication.** Do not report sensitive concerns in repository Issues, PRs, reviews or Project items; their audience can become public. The old private-stage issue route is not a confidential public reporting channel.

The maintainer must approve an actual confidential contact, verify that reports can be received, and identify a separate person/channel for concerns involving the maintainer and for independent reconsideration. No alternate person or contact has been verified for this transition. Do not infer one from a GitHub profile or send sensitive details through an unverified route. See the [source visibility checklist](docs/public-readiness.md) for this deferred release/collaboration gate.

## Response and enforcement

The maintainer reviews reports in context, limits unnecessary sharing, and explains any action to affected participants when practical. Available actions include asking for a correction, removing inappropriate content, ending an interaction, or restricting participation. Consider severity, repeated behavior, and safety; do not promise a fixed response time or confidentiality that the reporting system cannot provide.

Participants may request reconsideration with additional evidence through the same appropriate reporting channel. An independent appeal route has not yet been established and must be resolved before public contribution intake or supported library/CLI publication. Self-review by an automated agent does not replace the maintainer's judgment on conduct matters.
